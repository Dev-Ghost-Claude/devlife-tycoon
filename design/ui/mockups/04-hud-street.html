Always on screen

  • Time, date, speed: top right. Numbers, not words.
  • Stamina: ten lit segments and a bolt. No label.
  • Money: coin and amount. Changes float up for 2 s.
  • Toast: one line, 4 s, bottom centre.

Only when needed

  • Knowledge: a chip under the portrait for 3 s after a change. Full view on Tab.
  • Next up: appears 2 h before an appointment.
  • Verb tag: follows the hand cursor over anything usable.
  • Saving cassette: blinks for 1 s on each autosave.

Rules used

  • 10 Labels last: icons and formats carry the meaning.
  • 6/9 Hierarchy: dark wells and bold numerals lead; the date and the hints go quiet.
  • 39 Flat depth: hard shadows, no blur.
  • 35 Not colour alone: stamina low also changes the bolt and blinks.
