Concept B: the reading (alternative)

  • A candle-lit table. Old Rook looms behind the cards as a dark shape. Three tall cards lie face up: The Fox, The Flat, The Pocket. It reads like a fortune, and it is the same hand.
  • Same facts, same Wake up, same seed. The cards are bigger and calmer than the board. Less craft per square inch, faster to build.
  • Reveal: three card backs (a woven thread pattern). The Game Master's shadow sweeps. Each card flips in 4 frames, with a 2 px bounce. The middle card lands 16 px higher.

Inspired by (ideas only)

  • Dealt hands in card roguelikes: a spring flip and a small shake.
  • A dealer hidden in the dark across a table.
  • Tarot readings: a name on each card and a reading under it.

Compare with the board (03)

  • The board has a stronger link to the Game Master's weave and more tactile objects. It costs more art.
  • The reading is closer to a normal card UI, and its three columns stay readable when a fourth card is added.
  • Open question 11 asks which to build.
