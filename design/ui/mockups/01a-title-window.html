Idea

  • The menu is the bedroom at night. Every menu item is a thing in the room.
  • Desk and glowing monitor = Continue. Bed = New life (wake up). Shelf of tapes = Hall of fame. Radio = Settings. Door = Quit.
  • The crow on the wire outside is a Game Master. It watches. It is the only moving thing.

Input

  • Mouse: hover lights an item with corner brackets and shows its tag. Click chooses.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys jump between items. Tags are always visible, so nothing depends on guessing.
  • Primary: Continue. First run: the bed glows and New life becomes primary.

Cost and risk

  • Needs a real 3D or 2D room scene with hit areas. About 3 to 4 times the work of concept B.
  • Strong mood. It reuses the Room state, so the engine already knows how to frame a room.
  • Rules used: 6 hierarchy (one rust tag), 9 quiet the rest, 14 space (the logo has air).
