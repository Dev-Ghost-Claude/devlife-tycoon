DevLife Tycoon: UI mockups

Revision 2. It starts the talk; it is not final. Each page is one screen at 1280 by 720, which is the 640 by 360 game canvas at 2x. Read UI_PROPOSAL.md for the screen map and the open questions.

Era: bedroom (beige plastic, 16 fixed colours)Type: Source Sans 3, Semibold and BoldPixels: 1 canvas pixel = 2 CSS px

Boot and menus

01a Title: the window at night

Concept A. The bedroom is the menu: desk is Continue, bed is New life, shelf is Hall of fame.

01b Title: the tape deck

Concept B. A flat cassette deck with transport keys. Your life is the tape.

New life

02 New life: choose a Game Master

Pick one animal narrator who weaves your story, then set the cruelty slider.

03 New life: the deal (the board)

The Game Master pins your random hand to the wall and weaves one red thread through it.

03b New life: the deal (alternative)

An alternative: a candle-lit reading, three tall cards, the Game Master looming behind.

In the world

04 HUD in the street

Bedroom-era HUD: time, stamina and money always; knowledge and hints on demand.

05 Pause menu

Esc menu. One big button: Resume. The life is already saved.

06 Game Master box

Old Rook speaks, cold and half garbled. The chill ladder shows how the box turns.

08 Scene pick

A key moment: three actions with sure costs and gates, and no odds.

Sheets, devices and news

12 Character sheet and journal

A 1978 ring binder with colour tabs: skills, status marks, a detail card, a map inset.

07 Home computer

Era-styled device: beige monitor, a page service, one primary action.

09 The morning paper

A folded, worn 1978 local paper: masthead, halftone photo, ads, classifieds, your pen marks.

The end

10 Post-mortem replay

After a game over: the life as a tape, with choices and omens seen or missed.

11 Legacy and obituary

A black-paper album: the epithet, the games, the people, a film strip of the eras. Continue as the heir.

If the type looks wrong when you open a file from disk, your browser blocked the font files in assets/fonts. Serve the game folder instead: run python3 -m http.server in bevy-game and open /design/ui/mockups/index.html.
