What it does

  • Step 1 of 3. Pick one Game Master, then set a separate cruelty slider.
  • Mouse: click a card, drag the knob. Keys: Left and Right pick a card, Up and Down move the slider, Enter goes on.
  • The first build ships with one awake card. The layout already holds three.

Wording

  • Each Game Master is a narrator who weaves your story. The card text and the quote speak in that voice.
  • The lock notice moved next to the button, where the choice is made: "Locked for the whole life."
  • The quote is cold on purpose. It shows how the chosen Game Master will sound.

Rules used

  • 6 Hierarchy: the chosen card is raised, has a rust top bar and a check. Asleep cards drop to putty.
  • 9 Quiet the rest: asleep cards use dithered silhouettes, a ribbon and muted text.
  • 46 Accent border: rust bar on the chosen card.
  • 35 Not colour alone: chosen = lift + bar + check.
