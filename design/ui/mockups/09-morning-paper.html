What it does

  • You pick up the paper at the door or at breakfast. Reading takes 10 game minutes. Space turns the page. Esc puts it down.
  • One lead story, small items, ads, classifieds. Hover a story to see the cut-here guide. Press C or click the chip to clip it into the Journal.
  • Omens hide in the small print. The classifieds hold a clue, and the pen mark is the player's own earlier clipping.

The look (1978 local paper)

  • A pixel masthead with ears, a heavy double rule and a dateline. Headlines run in four sizes: 36, 24, 16 and 14.
  • The photo is a 4x4 ordered halftone in two inks. Ads have their own voices: a pixel logo and a coupon, an inverted box, a boxed line.
  • The sheet is folded twice and worn: a torn corner, specks, a coffee ring that matches the mug on the desk, a pen loop in blue ink.
  • Later eras swap the whole object: TV bulletin, web page, social feed. The slots stay: lead, small items, clip.

Rules used

  • 6 Hierarchy: masthead, lead, deck, body, in clear steps. Only the Put the paper down button is rust-filled.
  • 22 Line length: the lead column is 256 px, about 40 characters. It is a newspaper column, so it is narrow on purpose.
  • 34 Contrast: every text sits on newsprint, cream or ink. The coffee ring sits under the text, so text is never on the ring alone.
  • 26 Left-aligned text. Only the masthead and the ears centre.
