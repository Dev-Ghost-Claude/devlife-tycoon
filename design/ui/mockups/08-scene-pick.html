What it does

  • A key moment. The world dims, the clock stops, and three actions appear as cards.
  • Click or press 1 to 3 to pick. The pick lifts. Enter or Do it confirms. No take-backs after that.
  • Esc opens the pause menu. The choice stays open.

What it shows

  • Sure things only: time, stamina, money, gates. The HUD stamina bar previews the loss as hatched segments.
  • Gates show as a chip with a lock, or a tick when you pass.
  • It never shows odds. Danger is in the world and the omens.

Rules used

  • 13 One primary: Do it. Cards are choices, not buttons.
  • 10 Labels last: costs read as 3 h and three bolts.
  • 35 Not colour alone: chosen = lift + rust bar. Hatched = preview.
  • 36 Raised card vs inset chips.
