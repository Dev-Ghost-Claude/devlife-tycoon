What it does

  • The life is judged on a black-paper album. Left page: who they were. Right page: the games and the people. Bottom: a film strip of the life across the seven eras, ending on the Game Master's last word.
  • Photos arrive page by page: the portrait first, then the clipping, the four game covers, the people, then the strip. Each lands with a small tape slap. The last frame types in.
  • Primary: Continue as Pip. With no heir the primary is Begin another life. Hall of fame is secondary. The title is a link.

Craft

  • One strong line: the epithet "Cult legend" in big bitmap letters, with a rust drop. Many titles exist, such as beloved auteur and industry titan.
  • Everything is mounted: photo corners, tape, a torn clipping, a sticky note from Pip, a coloured film strip with sprocket holes. The text is short and plain.
  • The strip shows the workplace growing through the eras, from the bedroom to the campus. The last frame is cold on purpose.

Rules used

  • 6 Hierarchy: the epithet is the biggest type. The primary is the only rust block.

  • 40 Overlap: tape, corners and clippings layer over the page.
