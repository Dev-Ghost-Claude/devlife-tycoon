Idea

  • Your life is a cassette. Continue plays it. New life records a new one. Hall of fame rewinds through old tapes.
  • The deck is flat UI: cheap to build, and it fits the beige era.
  • The counter shows the days of this life. The display whispers when the Game Master greets you again.

Input

  • Mouse: click a key. Keyboard: Left and Right move focus, Enter presses, Esc does nothing.
  • Primary: Continue. With no tape in the deck, Continue is gone and New life becomes the primary.
  • Later eras swap the deck: CD player, console, phone lock screen.

Rules used

  • 13 One primary key, larger and rust. Others are plastic. Settings and Quit are smaller still.
  • 36 Raised keys: light top edge, dark base. The counter well is inset.
  • 27 Caps get extra tracking. 21 One font family, two weights.
  • 14 Space first: the deck uses 880 of 1280 px.
