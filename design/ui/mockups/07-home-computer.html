Stable core (never re-skinned)

  • The HUD stays: time, stamina, money. They sit outside the monitor.
  • Esc or a click outside leans back. Time keeps running while you read.
  • Icons keep their meaning: bolt = stamina cost, lock = a gate you cannot pass yet.

Era skin (bedroom, 1978)

  • Beige monitor, stepped CRT corners, scan lines, striped window title bars.
  • Teletext-style page numbers: 100 Jobs, 200 News, 300 Shops. It can only read pages.
  • Later eras swap this whole frame: CRT green, glossy 2000s, flat. Same layout slots.

Rules used

  • 13 One primary: Call about it. Everything else is quiet.
