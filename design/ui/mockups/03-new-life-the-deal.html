Concept: the board

  • Old Rook pins your life to your bedroom wall and weaves one red thread through it: you, your home, your pocket, then the alarm clock.
  • The thread is the reading order. It matches the Game Master line from step 1: "I hold the other end."
  • The same facts as before: animal, name, background, traits; home and house rules; computer, money, fund, bike. The seed is a tag on the shelf. There is no redeal.

Reveal, in order (animate-ready)

  • 0.0 s: the board is bare. The crow lands on the frame (3 frames). A lamp pool flickers on.
  • 0.5 s: the thread runs to pin 1, one canvas pixel a frame. The school photo slaps down: 6 px high, 0, minus 2, 0. The board shakes 2 px for 2 frames. A tick sound rises in pitch for each pin.
  • 0.9 s: the NERD sticker stamps on at 2x, then 1x. The three trait lines write in one by one.
  • 1.8 s and 3.0 s: pins 2 and 3 land the same way. The thread runs down the right side to the clock.
  • 3.8 s: the clock bells tremble (2-frame shake) and Wake up un-greys. A click anywhere before this fast-forwards the whole reveal.
  • Tilts are drawn into the sprites. Never rotate the textures.

Rules used

  • 6 Hierarchy: Wake up is the only rust block. The pocket card is the quietest paper.
  • 40 Overlap: slips over polaroids, pins over thread, the clock over the frame.
  • 10 Labels last: house rules read as 22:00 and 1 hour a day. Tick and cross shapes carry traits.
  • 34 Contrast: all text sits on cream or on the dark shelf. Inspired by: dealt hands and tactile tables in card roguelikes, document desks, evidence boards.
